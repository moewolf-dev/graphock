# Free and Pro

Graphock is free to download for the Direct build, and Pro is a single one-time purchase, not a subscription. The Mac App Store edition works differently: it is a one-time paid app and every local feature is included.

## Comparison

| | Direct Free | Direct Pro | Mac App Store |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Local parsing with the built-in engine | Included | Included | Included |
| Apple Vision OCR and Apple native document reader | Included | Included | Included |
| Files, workspace and local settings | Included | Included | Included |
| Graphock account at launch | Required | Required | Not required |
| Cloud parsing | Signed in, monthly allowance | Same allowance; Pro does not remove it | Signed in, monthly allowance |
| Tasks page, Templates, Graph, File Galaxy, Reports | Not included | Included | Included |
| Community templates download | Not included | Included | Included |
| Windows build | Available | Available | Not available |
| Price | Free | One-time purchase on graphock.com | One-time App Store price |

Pro requires a Graphock account in the Direct build because the entitlement is verified per account. Pro does not include unlimited cloud processing, priority queueing or additional storage.

## Cloud allowance

A signed-in account has a monthly cloud parsing allowance. It is currently 50 calls per UTC month and does not carry over. Pro does not raise this allowance today; any promotion or plan change is shown in the app and by the service. The allowance follows the Graphock account, so signing in on another device uses the same allowance.

## Buying Pro

**Status: coming soon for the Direct build.** The one-time website purchase is not on sale yet. The Mac App Store edition is a separate paid app with its own App Store listing.

- The website shows the price at checkout. This guide does not quote an amount for the Direct purchase, because a price is only valid once the live checkout and product configuration have been verified.
- The App Store edition is paid for through the App Store, includes every local feature and has nothing to buy inside the app.
- A Direct Pro purchase and the App Store edition are separate licences. Buying one does not unlock the other.

See [Buying, restoring and editions](./buy-and-restore.md).

## Refunds and old subscriptions

- A full Direct refund revokes Pro for that purchase. Other purchases on the same account are unaffected.
- App Store refunds are handled by Apple.
- Graphock no longer sells new subscriptions. Existing subscriptions, orders and invoices are not cancelled or erased by the one-time product. Their renewal, payment-failure and cancellation events continue to be processed.
