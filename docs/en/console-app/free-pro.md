# Free and Pro

Graphock is free to download and use for local work. Pro is a single one-time purchase, not a subscription.

## Comparison

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local parsing in a local workspace | Included | Included |
| Files, workspace and local settings | Included | Included |
| Cloud parsing | With a signed-in account, within the monthly allowance | Same allowance; Pro does not remove it |
| Tasks page, Templates, Graph, File Galaxy, Reports | Not included | Included |
| Community templates download | Not included | Included (sign-in required) |
| Price | Free | Shown at checkout when purchasing is available |

Pro requires a Graphock account because the entitlement is verified per account. Pro does not include unlimited cloud processing, priority queueing or additional storage.

## Cloud allowance

A signed-in account has a monthly cloud parsing allowance. It is currently 50 calls per UTC month and does not carry over. Pro does not raise this allowance today; any promotion or plan change is shown in the app and by the service. The allowance is shared across devices and channels that use the same Graphock account.

## Buying Pro

**Status: coming soon.** The one-time purchase is not on sale yet.

- A Direct purchase and a Mac App Store purchase must be associated with the same Graphock account to share Pro.

## Refunds and old subscriptions

- A full refund revokes Pro for that purchase. Other purchases on the same account are unaffected.
- Graphock no longer sells new subscriptions. Existing subscriptions, orders and invoices are not cancelled or erased by the one-time product. Their renewal, payment-failure and cancellation events continue to be processed.
