# Getting Started

Welcome to Graphock! This guide will help you get up and running in minutes.

## What is Graphock?

Graphock is a powerful data visualization platform that enables you to:

- 🎨 Create stunning charts and graphs from your data
- 🔍 Analyze trends, patterns, and insights
- 👥 Share and collaborate with your team
- 📱 Access your visualizations anywhere

## Before You Begin

### System Requirements

Graphock works entirely in the cloud - no installation required! All you need is:

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- A stable internet connection
- A free Graphock account

## Quick Overview

### 1. Sign Up

[Create your free account](/getting-started/sign-up) to get started. It only takes a minute!

### 2. Import Your Data

Connect to spreadsheets, databases, or APIs. We support:

- Excel and CSV files
- JSON data
- Databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL, etc.)
- Web APIs

### 3. Create Visualizations

Use our drag-and-drop interface to build charts, graphs, and dashboards.

### 4. Share Insights

Publish your work and collaborate with team members in real-time.

## What Can I Visualize?

Graphock can handle:

- Small datasets (dozens of rows)
- Medium datasets (thousands of rows)
- Large datasets (millions of rows)

### Recommended Data Formats

- **Structured Data**: Tables with clear headers
- **Time Series Data**: Dates and timestamps
- **Categorical Data**: Groups and categories
- **Geographic Data**: Locations and coordinates
