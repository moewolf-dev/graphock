# FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Graphock.

## General Questions

### What is Graphock?

Graphock is a data visualization platform designed for business analysts, researchers, and anyone who needs to transform data into actionable insights.

### Is Graphock free?

Yes! Graphock offers a free plan for individuals and small projects. We also offer Pro and Enterprise plans with additional features.

### What browsers are supported?

Graphock works with all modern web browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

## Data & Visualizations

### What file formats are supported?

Graphock supports CSV, Excel, JSON, and TSV files. We also connect to databases and APIs.

### How much data can I import?

Free plan: Up to 50,000 rows
Pro plan: Up to 1,000,000 rows
Enterprise: Unlimited

### What chart types are available?

We support bar charts, line charts, network graphs, scatter plots, pie charts, maps, and more.

## Account & Billing

### How do I upgrade my plan?

Go to Settings > Billing in your account to view and change your plan.

### Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel at any time. Your plan will remain active until the end of your billing period.
